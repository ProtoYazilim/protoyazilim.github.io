1. Who we are

Cross Math LEO is published by Proto Yazılım.

  • Website: www.protoyazilim.com
  • Android package name: com.protoyazilim.crossmath
  • iOS bundle identifier: com.protoyazilim.crossmath

2. Information stored on your device

To keep your game between sessions, the app stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Level progress, stars, and completed sections
  • Energy balance and remaining hints
  • Settings: language, sound effects, and music
  • Whether Remove Ads is active
  • Counters that decide when to suggest a store review or sharing the game
  • Your advertising-consent status, where the operating system keeps it
  • A clock offset used only so refill timers stay fair if the device clock changes

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this information from the device. We do not collect precise GPS location.

3. Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how levels are played and to improve the game. Events can include starting a level, completing or failing a level, stars earned, difficulty, and level type. Firebase may also process an app-instance identifier, device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and approximate country. We do not use these events to identify you by name.

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes. A report can include the error, a stack trace, device and system details, the app version, and the last level you were playing. We use crash reports to fix bugs.

More information: Firebase privacy information.

4. Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, including banners, rewarded ads (for example to earn extra energy), and ads that may appear when you return to the game.

  • On iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt asks permission before the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used to track you across apps and websites. If you decline, we do not use the IDFA for that tracking.
  • On Android, and where required in the EEA, the United Kingdom, and similar regions, Google’s consent form lets you choose personalized or non-personalized ads and manage privacy options.
  • If you buy Remove Ads, in-game advertising is turned off and we do not ask for ad-tracking consent for that purpose.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, and ad interactions under their own policies to deliver, measure, and secure ads, and to personalize them where allowed.

Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

5. Purchases and game services

Remove Ads is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We learn whether the purchase is active. We do not receive your card number or full payment details.

You can optionally sign in with Apple Game Center or Google Play Games. If you do, scores such as total stars and completed levels, and achievements, are submitted to that service and shown on its leaderboards under your platform profile. You can play without signing in. Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy applies to that account.

6. Notifications, time check, and sharing

Notifications. If you allow them, the game schedules local reminders on your device. They are not sent from our servers. A reminder is planned for about 13:00 in your local time after roughly 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, and 45 days without opening the game. Opening the game resets that schedule. The time zone is read on the device only to choose that hour. You can turn notifications off in system settings.

Time check. Refill timers may request the current time from a public time service (worldtimeapi.org) or from the date header of an HTTPS response. That request can reveal your IP address to the time service. We do not send your name, progress, or advertising identifier with it. If the network is unavailable, the device clock is used.

Sharing. The share button opens your system share sheet. We do not receive the recipients of that message.

7. How we use information

  • Run the game, save progress, and deliver purchases
  • Show ads and respect your consent choices
  • Measure gameplay so levels can be balanced
  • Diagnose crashes and improve stability
  • Offer optional local reminders
  • Keep refill timers consistent

We do not sell your personal information.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we rely on performance of the game you choose to play, our legitimate interest in keeping the game stable and fair, and consent where it is required (including personalized ads, cross-app tracking, and notifications). In Türkiye, we process personal data in line with the KVKK (Law No. 6698) on the corresponding grounds of contract, legitimate interest, and explicit consent where consent is required.

8. Children

Cross Math LEO is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13, or under the higher minimum age that applies in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Retention, international transfers, and your rights

Information stored on the device remains until you delete the app or clear its storage. Analytics, crash, and advertising data are retained by Google according to Firebase and AdMob settings. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google.

Google and Apple may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, they use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict personal information, to object to certain processing, to receive a copy, and to withdraw consent. You can:

  • Delete on-device data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage
  • On iOS, decline or withdraw tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • On Android, manage ad choices in Google’s consent and ads settings, and in the privacy-options form when the game shows it
  • Turn notifications off in system settings
  • Sign out of Game Center or Play Games in the platform’s settings
  • Request a refund from Apple or Google for a purchase

For other requests, contact us through our website. Because we do not keep a named player profile of our own, we may need Google or Apple to act on data they hold.

Store and service traffic uses encrypted connections. Data on the device stays in the app’s private storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or the law changes. The date at the top of this page will change, and the new version applies when it is published here.
