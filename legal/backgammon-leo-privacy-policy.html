Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Türkçe · English

Thank you for using Back Gammon Leo (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how Proto Yazılım (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your personal data. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information: When you create an account or sign in to play online, your email address and authentication credentials (password or one-time email code). Passwords are not stored by us in plain text; they are handled securely by our authentication provider.
  • Profile information: Your chosen nickname, optional skill label, online rating, and match statistics (wins, streaks, etc.).
  • Gameplay and matchmaking data: Room / invite codes, match state, move and dice events, opponent pairings, and records needed for the leaderboard. Match history may be retained in anonymized form after account deletion.
  • Preferences stored on device: Language, theme, sound, and gameplay settings stored locally on your device (e.g. SharedPreferences); these are not required to be tied to an online account.
  • Product analytics: Anonymous or aggregated event records about feature use (e.g. online match started, joker used). These events do not include free text or direct identifiers; they may be sent to our servers while you are signed in.
  • Advertising data: When in-app ads are shown, the ad provider (Google AdMob) may process device and advertising identifiers and ad interaction data under its own policies.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create your account, sign you in, and verify your identity.
  • To provide online multiplayer backgammon, matchmaking, invite links, and the leaderboard.
  • To show and update your profile, rating, and statistics.
  • To improve app performance and feature usage.
  • To display in-app ads (via a third-party advertising SDK).
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following services:

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.): Authentication, database, realtime game events, and product analytics storage. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): In-app advertising. Privacy Policy · AdMob and privacy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: App distribution and store services are subject to their own privacy terms.

Friend-invite share links may open static pages hosted on www.protoyazilim.com to show the invite code and help open the App Store / Play Store or the installed app.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Account data is kept while your account is active. To delete your account, open the app, go to your online profile, and confirm Delete account. After confirmation, your account and personal profile data are deleted; match history may remain anonymized. Local settings on your device may remain until you uninstall the app or clear app data.

5. Data Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, and our authentication provider’s security measures to protect your data. No method is 100% secure; we apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.

6. Children’s Privacy

Back Gammon Leo is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / applicable law)

Under applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing
  • Request data portability

To exercise these rights, contact info@protoyazilim.com or use the in-app account deletion feature.

8. International Transfers

Our service providers (e.g. Supabase, Google) may process data outside your country. In that case, their contractual and legal safeguards apply.

9. Policy Changes

When we update this policy, we will revise the date on this page. For material changes, we will try to provide reasonable notice.
